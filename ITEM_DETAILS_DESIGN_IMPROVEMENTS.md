## 🎨 Design System

### Color Palette
- **Primary Blue**: `#3266a8` → `#2a5490` (gradient)
- **Lost Item**: `#ff6b6b` → `#ee5a52` (red gradient)
- **Found Item**: `#51cf66` → `#40c057` (green gradient)
- **AI Purple**: `#9c27b0` (for AI features)
- **Background**: `#f8f9fa`
- **Text**: `#1a202c` (dark) / `#4a5568` (medium) / `#718096` (light)

### Typography
- **Title**: 26px, weight 800
- **Section Headers**: 17px, weight 700
- **Body Text**: 15px, weight 500
- **Labels**: 12px, weight 600, uppercase
- **Buttons**: 15px, weight 700

### Spacing
- **Card Padding**: 20px
- **Card Margin**: 16px
- **Container Padding**: 20px
- **Icon Container**: 40x40px
- **Border Radius**: 16px (cards), 12px (buttons), 20px (tags)

### Shadows & Elevations
- **Cards**: elevation 3, subtle shadows
- **Buttons**: elevation 4, colored shadows
- **Header**: elevation 8 when scrolled
- **Badges**: elevation 6, prominent shadows
